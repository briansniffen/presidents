# Presidents Flashcards

Flashcards for the 47 presidencies of the United States: years on the front; name, party, predecessor and successor on the back.

- Each review runs through the cards **in order**: all 47, or a group of ten.
- Grade each card Missed, Shaky or Knew it. Shaky cards come back once more; missed cards come back until they're right twice.
- Repeats come back **in sets**: the card before each weak one runs first as a lead-in, then the weak card, all in order, round after round until everything is known.
- "Trouble spots" remembers what was missed or shaky in past reviews and runs just those, with their lead-ins.
- Progress is stored on the device (no account) and survives closing the app. Works offline and can be added to a phone's home screen.

Plain static files; no build step. Served with GitHub Pages.
