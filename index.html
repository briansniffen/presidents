    How it works: each review goes through the cards in order. Anything marked Shaky comes back once more, and anything Missed comes back until it's right twice. Repeats come back in sets: the card before it first, as a lead-in, then the one to work on, all in order, round after round, until every card is a Knew it. Trouble spots remember what's been missed or shaky in past reviews, so you can run just those. Everything is saved on this device; close the app and it picks up where you left off.
